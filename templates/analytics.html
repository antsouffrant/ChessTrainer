Headline approval rates are rating-based; category approval charts count distinct rated positions. Historical-version filters apply to feedback recorded under that version; the three taxonomy change-rate cards always describe the current solved-position classifications, not historical reconstructed predictions. Taxonomy change rate = solved positions whose current selected label differs from the current coach label ÷ all solved positions in the selected game-list/position filters. Untouched positions and selections changed back to the coach label count as unchanged. Approval rate = thumbs-up ratings ÷ all thumbs ratings in that area. Taxonomy change is a separate current-position metric; neither rate implies the other. Feedback-type and rating filters do not change the solved-position denominator.

Coaching development dataset

Export thumbs for position relevance, explanations and chat, plus taxonomy selections with both the at-selection coach prediction and the latest current comparison. Earlier retired-category ratings are retained in CSV for backward compatibility. Each CSV row has a stable position ID and the evidence needed to revisit the decision.

Coach versions are recorded at feedback time for new ratings and taxonomy selections. Older chat/taxonomy records without a saved version use the current position version. Refreshing metadata does not remove historical feedback snapshots. Ratings are the latest vote per item. The content_snapshot is what was rated; current_explanation and current motif fields may have changed after model revisions. The export includes suggested_text, suggested_motif_key, and suggested_motif_name for downvotes, alongside FENs and engine variations (but not complete PGNs). Switching a rating to thumbs-up clears its correction. Regression candidates include corrected taxonomy choices and downvotes with saved corrections; they are user-proposed examples, not independently verified chess truth.
