Personal Chess Trainer

Turn your own chess games into a personalized course in better decision-making.

How it works

  1. Import games from Lichess or PGN and choose analysis settings.
  2. Explore openings by opening family, variation, and move number.
  3. Explore lessons by the most important teachable concept in each position; secondary motifs are still shown after solving.
  4. Train by watching the preceding move and finding a strong response. Request hints, analyze variations, and mark positions learned.
  5. Track progress and resume a saved lesson playlist.

Learning modes describe performance; learning motifs describe the main idea. Estimated difficulty ratings are provisional heuristics, not validated Elo.
